# Sprinkle Hollow

A cozy town game: make your character, pick a buddy (unicorn, dog, cat or dino), help your neighbors, decorate your house, and reach Level 5 to open the Rainbow Gate.

## Play it

**https://chasejonez.github.io/sparkle-hollow-/**

Every push to `main` builds and publishes the game there automatically (see `.github/workflows/deploy.yml`). The build number is shown at the bottom of the ⚙️ menu, so you can check which version a device is running.

### On a phone or tablet
- Open the link in Safari (iPhone/iPad) or Chrome (Android).
- To play full screen like an app: **Share → Add to Home Screen** (iPhone/iPad) or **⋮ → Add to Home screen** (Android).
- Saves are kept per device and per browser, so each device has its own game.

### Controls
- **Touch:** drag on the left side of the screen to walk; tap the big button to talk, go inside, use things, or pet your buddy.
- **Keyboard:** WASD or arrow keys to walk; Space / E / Enter for the big button.

## Files
- `index.html` — the whole game (no build step, no dependencies)
- `manifest.webmanifest`, `icon-*.png`, `apple-touch-icon.png` — home-screen app icon and settings
